# Contributing to PLANNR

PLANNR is licensed under the GNU AGPL, version 3 only. See `LICENSE` and
`COPYRIGHT`.

## Contributor agreement

Every contribution is covered by `CLA.md`. Opening a pull request, or
sending a patch, means you agree to that agreement.

Denny Moritz needs the agreement so the project can stay under the AGPL and
also be offered under other terms, including a commercial license, and so
those rights can move with the project if it is sold.

Copyright in your contribution stays with you.

## Scope

Issues and pull requests are for the self-hosted application: the desktop
app, the mobile app, the server, and the container build.

Do not send lab notes, private calendars, passwords, or machine-specific
configuration.
